How papers are counted. First-author country uses the first author’s first affiliation (about 9% of institutions could not be mapped reliably). “Any author” counts a paper if at least one author is affiliated with that country; a paper can count for several countries. Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan are listed separately from mainland China.

Country share

Mainland China has 1,132 first-author papers (39.9%), 2.7× the United States (424, 15.0%). Greater China is 46.5% of first-author papers and 56.4% of papers with at least one Greater China author. South Korea is third by first author (9.2%).

Sweden, Netherlands, France

Host-country Sweden has only 11 first-author papers (0.4%). Sample sizes are small, so percentages are structural signals: Sweden is half geometry, the Netherlands leans embodied and 3D, France leans generation and theory.

Ten areas with the largest deviations. Swedish geometry is +40 pp versus the conference — the opposite of China’s slight under-index on geometry.

Sweden: half geometry

9 of 20 papers are geometry / localization / matching (45% vs 5% conference-wide): RoMa v2, LoMa, SynFlow, PolyLayout. Generation and embodied AI are almost absent.

Netherlands: embodied + 3D

Embodied / driving is 16.1% (conference 6.8%). Amsterdam leans models and VLMs; Delft leans matching and driving. Video understanding is low.

France: generation and theory

Video generation 14.3%. Robustness / theory, registration, segmentation, and low-level restoration are each about 10%. Only one embodied paper.

Uncertainty / conformal / calibration

There is no official uncertainty area. Titles that name UQ, conformal prediction, evidential methods, or confidence calibration are about 1.0% of the conference. “Uncertainty” in the abstract is 3.5%. Conformal prediction appears in only three papers.
